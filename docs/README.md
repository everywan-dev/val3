# Documents

- `design/`: the design (`2026-10-08-val3-design.md`) and one design per feature.
- `adr/`: the decisions, numbered.
- `plans/`: the implementation plans, as written and run, with the results of their measurements.
- `research/`: measurements and comparisons that are not tied to one plan.

These are the working documents of development. Where they cite `TODO.md`, `SESSION.md`, `sessions/` or a kit
under `~/.local/share/val3/eval/`, those are the development log and the measurement kits of the private
development repository: they hold the adopting projects' own sessions and are not published. What a result needs
to be understood is in the plan or the research document that reports it.
